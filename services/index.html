Tests and services

Digital imaging, a full laboratory and a 24-hour blood centre. Each test below says when it is available and how to prepare.

Dr Kajli Gupta Digital Imaging Centre

Digital imaging

Digital X-ray

9 AM to 6 PM

Digital radiography for the chest, bones, joints, spine, sinuses and abdomen. Images are ready within minutes and use a lower radiation dose than film.

Before you come

Remove metal jewellery and belts. Tell the staff if you are, or might be, pregnant.

Book on WhatsApp

3D / 4D ultrasound

10 AM to 4 PM

Pregnancy scans in 3D and 4D, including dating, anomaly and growth scans, along with ultrasound of the abdomen, pelvis, thyroid, breast and soft tissues. Safe, with no radiation.

Before you come

Lower abdomen and pelvis scans need a full bladder. Upper abdomen scans need about 6 hours of fasting. Bring earlier reports.

Book on WhatsApp

Colour Doppler

10 AM to 4 PM

Ultrasound that maps blood flow: fetal well-being in pregnancy, leg veins and arteries, neck (carotid) vessels and kidneys.

Before you come

Usually no preparation. You will be told if fasting is needed.

Book on WhatsApp

Fetal echocardiography

10 AM to 4 PM

A detailed ultrasound study of the baby's heart structure and rhythm during pregnancy, usually advised between 18 and 24 weeks.

Before you come

No special preparation. Please bring your earlier pregnancy scans.

Book on WhatsApp

Neurosonogram

10 AM to 4 PM

Ultrasound of an infant's brain through the soft spot (fontanelle). Quick, painless and free of radiation.

Before you come

Feed the baby shortly before the scan so that they stay calm.

Book on WhatsApp

Mammography

9 AM to 6 PM

Screening and diagnostic mammography to find breast changes early, when they are easiest to treat.

Before you come

Avoid deodorant, powder or cream on the day. The week after your period is the most comfortable time.

Book on WhatsApp

Digital OPG

9 AM to 6 PM

Panoramic dental X-ray for dentists and orthodontists: all teeth, both jaws and the joints in a single image.

Before you come

Remove earrings, spectacles, hairpins and removable dentures.

Book on WhatsApp

Janta Pathology

Laboratory

Pathology

8 AM to 8 PM

Haematology, clinical pathology, cytology and histopathology (biopsy) reporting under the supervision of Dr Samir Gupta.

Before you come

Most blood tests need no preparation. Ask us when you book.

Book on WhatsApp

Biochemistry

8 AM to 8 PM

Blood sugar and HbA1c, kidney and liver function, lipid profile, thyroid profile, electrolytes and other routine and special tests.

Before you come

Fasting sugar and lipid profile need 10 to 12 hours of fasting. Plain water is allowed.

Book on WhatsApp

Microbiology

8 AM to 8 PM

Culture and sensitivity testing of urine, blood, pus and other samples, so your doctor can choose the right antibiotic.

Before you come

Give the sample before starting antibiotics if possible. For urine, a first-morning midstream sample in a sterile container.

Book on WhatsApp

Home sample collection

8 AM to 8 PM

A trained phlebotomist visits your home to collect samples, which is helpful for elderly or unwell patients.

Before you come

Call or WhatsApp to book a visit. Keep the doctor's prescription ready.

Book on WhatsApp

Blood Centre

24 hours

Licensed blood centre with component separation, quality-controlled testing and temperature-controlled storage. Open round the clock.

Before you come

For a blood request, bring the doctor's requisition form and the patient's blood sample as advised by the hospital.
