How we keep blood safe

Every unit, handled with care.

Component separation

Whole blood is separated into red cells, plasma and platelets, so one donation can help more than one patient.

Quality-controlled testing

Every unit is screened as required by national guidelines before it is issued.

Temperature-controlled storage

Dedicated refrigerators and plasma freezers keep each component at its correct temperature.

Trained team, 24 × 7

Trained staff look after donors and handle requests round the clock.

Donate blood रक्तदान करें

One donation can help more than one patient.

Your body replaces the donated blood within a few weeks, and you can go back to normal work the same day.

Who can donate

Age18 to 65 years
WeightAt least 45 kg (350 ml donation); over 55 kg for 450 ml
HaemoglobinAt least 12.5 g/dL
Gap between donations3 months for men, 4 months for women
On the dayFeeling well, no fever, normal pulse and blood pressure

Based on the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare Guidelines for Blood Donor Selection (National Blood Transfusion Council, 2017). Our medical officer makes the final decision on the day.

What happens when you donate

  1. 1

    Register

    A short form and a few questions about your health.

  2. 2

    Quick check

    Weight, pulse, blood pressure and haemoglobin are checked.

  3. 3

    Donate

    The donation itself takes about 5 to 10 minutes.

  4. 4

    Rest and refresh

    Relax for a few minutes with a drink and a snack.

Requesting blood

What to bring.

What do I need to request blood?

The treating doctor's requisition form with the patient's details, and the patient's blood sample as advised by the hospital, so that grouping and cross-matching can be done.

Which components are available?

Component separation means whole blood is separated into red cells, plasma and platelets. Call us to check what is available for your patient right now.

Can I donate in place of the blood my patient receives?

Replacement donation by family and friends helps keep the blood centre stocked for everyone. Please ask our staff when you call.

Is the Blood Centre open at night and on holidays?

Yes, it works 24 hours a day. Call 0591-3598163.
